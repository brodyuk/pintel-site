Pintel

Privacy notice

Last updated 10 November 2026

Pintel shows drink prices at pubs near you. This notice explains what we collect, why, who helps us run Pintel, how long we keep things and your rights. Questions: hello@getpintel.com.

What we collect

Your account: your email address if you sign in, or a guest account with no email if you just add a price. Your display name, points and badges.

What you add: prices, menu photos, confirmations and reports, with the time and the pub.

Your location: used on your phone to find pubs, stops and stations near you, and to check you are at a pub when you add or confirm a price. We do not keep a history of where you have been.

Saved pubs and deal messages: the pubs you save, your message settings, and, if you choose "Saved and nearby", a rough area (to about 1 km). If you turn messages on, your phone's notification address.

Usage counts: a random code made on your device and a few actions (for example "searched" or "viewed a pub"), so we can see whether Pintel is useful. No names or locations are attached.

Pub page views: we count how many times each pub's page is viewed each day, without recording who viewed it.

If you claim a pub: your name, role, phone number and email, so we can check you work there.

Why we use it

To run Pintel for you (our contract with you): your account, showing prices, saving pubs, your points.

To keep prices honest (our legitimate interests): checking you are at the pub, spotting wrong prices and misuse.

To improve Pintel (our legitimate interests): the usage counts and page views, which are not linked to you by name.

Deal messages (your consent): only if you turn them on. You can turn them off at any time under Deal messages, or in your phone's settings.

Who helps us run Pintel

Supabase stores Pintel's data and runs its server. Expo delivers the app, its updates and deal messages.

Map searches go to Photon (komoot), with map data from OpenStreetMap. Nearby stops and live departures in London come from Transport for London. These receive the location being searched, not who you are.

When you scan a menu, the photo may be sent to Google's Gemini service to read the prices. Google may use what it receives to improve its services.

getpintel.com uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

What others can see

Prices you add are public, with the time they were added. Leaderboards show your display name and points. Your email address is never shown.

How long we keep it

Your account, until you ask us to delete it. Prices stay after they expire, without your name, so price history remains accurate.

Usage counts: up to 2 years. Deal message records: 12 months. Pub claims: as long as you manage the pub, then 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent. Email hello@getpintel.com and we will reply within a month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Age

Pintel is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change applies.
