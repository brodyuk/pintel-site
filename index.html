Questions

Is Pintel free?

Yes, free for drinkers. Pubs get 6 months of free advertising, then pay £5 a month only if they want to carry on.

Where does it work?

We’re starting in Medway. Pubs elsewhere in the UK are on the map too, and prices fill in as people add them.

Where do the prices come from?

From drinkers who add them at the pub, from menu photos, and from pubs themselves. Other drinkers confirm prices, and anything out of date is marked.

Is it on iPhone?

Open pintel.expo.app in Safari, tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”. It then opens like an app. On Android, open it in Chrome and tap “Install app”. The App Store and Google Play versions are coming soon.

Will Pintel send me lots of notifications?

No. Messages are off unless you turn them on, and then it’s at most one a day and three a week, only for real deals at pubs you choose, never in your quiet hours.

I run a pub. How do I get listed?

You probably already are. Open Pintel, find your pub, and tap “Is this your pub?”. If we’ve emailed you, just reply with a photo of your drinks menu.
